Whispra

Whispra is a real-time voice translation app built for gamers, streamers, and teams who need instant cross-language communication. Speak in your own language — Whispra translates and plays it back instantly so everyone understands in real time.

🚀 Features

🎙️ Real-time voice translation — Talk naturally, Whispra handles the rest.

🕹️ Game & Overlay Mode — Works seamlessly while gaming, with an optional in-game overlay.

🔄 Bidirectional Translation — Translate both your voice and incoming audio.

⚡ Low Latency — Designed for fast, natural conversations without lag.

🔒 Secure — API keys are user-owned, and no data is stored without your permission.
